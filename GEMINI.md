# ZayedReviews

A personal review website for Zayed to share thoughts on shows, games, and various subjects.

## Vision & Style
- **Project Name:** ZayedReviews
- **Aesthetic:** Indie Web / Personal Journal. Not corporate, not "clean-modern".
- **Feel:** Expressive, unique, and content-focused.
- **Tech Stack:** React (Vite), TypeScript, Vanilla CSS.

## Content Categories
- **Shows:** TV series and movies.
- **Games:** Past, present, and future plays.
- **Opinions:** General thoughts and essays.

## Development Mandates
- Prioritize CSS for styling (no Tailwind).
- Maintain a "handmade" feel with custom layouts.
- Keep the code simple and easy for Zayed to add new reviews.
